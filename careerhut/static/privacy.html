Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 9, 2026 • Careerhut Direct Career Discovery Platform

🔒 Summary: Careerhut respects your privacy. We do not sell user data, track you with third-party advertising cookies, or store your personal bookmarks on central servers without consent.

1. Information We Collect

Careerhut is committed to data minimization. When you use our service:

  • Client-Side Storage: Bookmarks, application statuses, and search filters are stored locally in your browser's localStorage.
  • Server Request Logs: Standard web server logs (IP address, user agent, request timestamp) are retained transiently for security and rate limiting.
  • Public Portal Data: All job postings and verified recruiter emails are parsed strictly from public employer career portals.

2. How We Use Information

Data processed by Careerhut is used solely to:

  • Provide live job searching and split-pane inspection capabilities.
  • Surface verified recruiter contacts for direct applicant outreach.
  • Protect the platform against malicious scraping and unauthorized access.

3. Cookies & Tracking

Careerhut operates without third-party tracking cookies or advertising pixels. Essential local storage is used solely to remember user interface preferences.

4. Employer & Data Subject Inquiries

To request updates or removal of any employer listing or contact address, contact privacy@careerhut.org.
