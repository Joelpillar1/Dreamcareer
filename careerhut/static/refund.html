Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: October 9, 2026 • Careerhut Direct Career Discovery Platform

💳 Summary: Careerhut is completely free for job seekers. For employer sponsorship and hiring spotlight services, we offer a 14-day transparent refund policy for unrendered promotional placements.

1. 100% Free Core Platform Guarantee for Job Seekers

Careerhut is an open-access direct tech job discovery directory. Job seekers are never charged any fees, subscription charges, or paywalls to:

  • Search, filter, and inspect verified software engineering and tech job postings.
  • Access direct company career portal ATS links and verified recruiter email contacts.
  • Save bookmarks and manage application statuses in local storage.

2. Employer Sponsorship & Featured Postings

For verified employers, hiring organizations, and recruitment partners who purchase custom spotlight placements, featured company showcases, or verified ATS indexing boosts:

  • 14-Day Refund Window: Employers may request a full refund within 14 calendar days of purchase if the sponsored campaign or listing has not yet been published or active on the live directory.
  • Technical Non-Delivery: If a technical failure prevents a purchased featured placement from being properly displayed or indexed for more than 48 consecutive hours during an active campaign, a pro-rated refund or equivalent campaign extension will be issued upon verification.
  • Active Campaigns: Once a featured campaign has successfully run and concluded its scheduled placement duration, payments are considered final and non-refundable.

3. Cancellation Policy

Employers may cancel recurring promotional sponsorships or scheduled campaign renewals at any time by contacting our billing team. Cancellations take effect at the conclusion of the currently active billing period, and no further charges will occur.

4. How to Request a Refund or Billing Support

To submit a refund request or resolve billing inquiries, please email our support team at billing@careerhut.org with your transaction reference or organization name. All requests are reviewed and responded to within 2 business days.
